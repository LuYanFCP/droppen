## Droppen App for macOS

`Droppen-0.2.125.dmg` — the desktop app (Apple silicon and Intel), signed with Developer ID and notarised by Apple. It needs macOS 13 or later. Open the DMG, drag Droppen to Applications, open it.

From 0.2.125 on, the app updates itself: it downloads each new release in the background and installs it the next time you quit, or at once with *Restart to Update*. Version 0.2.124 and older do not update themselves: download this DMG once and replace the app in Applications.

## macOS 版 Droppen App

`Droppen-0.2.125.dmg` 是桌面 App（Apple 芯片和 Intel 芯片的 Mac 都能用），使用 Developer ID 签名并经过 Apple 公证，需要 macOS 13 或更高版本。打开 DMG，把 Droppen 拖到「应用程序」，然后打开它。

从 0.2.125 起，App 会自动更新：在后台下载每个新版本，并在你下次退出时安装；也可以用「重启以更新」立即安装。0.2.124 及更早的版本不会自动更新：请手动下载一次这个 DMG，替换「应用程序」里的 Droppen。

## What's new in v0.2.125

### Droppen App for macOS

- **The app now updates itself.** It checks for a new release after launch and every six hours, downloads it in the background (not on a metered network), checks its signature, and installs it the next time you quit. *Restart to Update* in the menu bar, or the note at the bottom of the window's side bar, installs it at once.
- **A first-run guide.** It gets Claude Code and Codex ready: installs or updates them (the official installer, Homebrew or npm; the exact command is shown before it runs), uses a copy already on this Mac, and signs them in. Then it asks where you will use Droppen: on your phone and other devices too (it signs this Mac in to your Droppen account in your browser), or only on this Mac. Last, it opens your first session filled in, in a recent project folder or a new practice folder; nothing runs until you press Send. *Finish later* leaves it; Settings → General → Setup opens it again.
- **Sign in to Droppen from the app**, in the guide or in Settings → Droppen: droppen.app opens in your browser, and once you add the device there, this Mac is signed in, without a restart.
- **Start at Login** is now a macOS login item: System Settings → Login Items lists it as Droppen, and it keeps working when the app is moved or renamed. It is on for new installs. An existing start-at-login setting is moved over once.
- **Open in Browser** signs the browser in with a one-time link.
- The server inside the app keeps its port across launches, so the page's sign-in and this Mac's device keys stay. A new port, when one is needed, comes from 18700–18759.
- The window no longer shows two empty screens copied from a command-line install, and the side bar's names no longer cover the window's close, minimise and zoom buttons.
- The app and its DMG include `LICENSE` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES`.
- The app now needs macOS 13 or later.

### Web app (droppen.app and the page on your device)

- **iPhone keyboard:** the message box no longer ends up under the keyboard. The page stopped measuring the keyboard and leaves it to Safari to bring the box into view.
- **My devices is one page:** usage at the top (your plan, transfer this period, cloud storage), this device's settings, then one list of all your devices with badges (can run AI, full device) and a menu on each. Browsers and phones are named after the device and browser, such as "iPhone · Safari". *Add a device* asks which kind first.
- **My account**, a new page (from the side bar and from My devices): your e-mail and how you sign in. Add, rename or remove passkeys; set, change or remove the password; your recovery words; sign out. A change made from one device shows a notice on your other devices. A new phone or browser signed in with a passkey can join with it, once another of your devices approves it through the usual pairing.
- **Every browser or phone you add is a full device:** the "can manage the account" choice is gone. When a device will not take this browser, the page says why instead of showing "connecting…" for ever.
- **The conversation only grows.** While Claude is thinking, the status bar shows it next to the message count ("thinking… ~50 tokens"), instead of a box in the conversation that kept changing height.
- **Device keys on iPhone and Safari:** a phone could pair and then not connect after a reload; its keys are now kept. A key that cannot be read for a moment no longer turns a phone into a new device: the page tries again, and if the keys still do not open, it asks before using this browser as a new device. When a browser does sign in again as a new device, the device that approves it offers to remove the old one.
- **Relays:** each of your devices that runs AI now stays connected to a relay even before a browser or phone has used one, and moves to another at once when its relay goes away. My devices has a new account setting, *Keep a standby relay*, for faster switching.
- A pairing that stops says why, with a next step that works (for example, sign in again when the 30 minutes after signing in have passed).
- The re-enroll warning for a computer names the devices that actually connected to it, and you can dismiss it.
- New accounts start on the current device list format, so no upgrade step shows for them.
- The pickers (model, effort and others) are the app's own menus instead of the system's, and open right against their control.
- New session: one centred column with the question, the message box and your recent folders; on a phone the message box sits at the bottom, as in a session.
- A message sent while Droppen is still choosing the account goes out exactly as you pressed it. What you type or attach afterwards stays in the box, and switching the AI tool cancels it.
- Security: a tab left open on an older copy of your device list can no longer roll back what other tabs verified; a page that finds two different device lists stops.

### Server (`droppen`)

- **The page on your device always asks you to sign in.** A device with no user account no longer opens for anyone without a session, not even on 127.0.0.1. On the device, run `droppen open` (or use the app's *Open in Browser*); from elsewhere, create the first account with the one-time setup code (`droppen setup-code`), or run `droppen user add <name>` on the device. Installs made with the install script already have an account and are not affected.
- The page can no longer be shown inside another site's frame. This also stops it opening in VS Code's or Cursor's Simple Browser: use a real browser.
- Signing out, revoking a sign-in or removing an account now also closes that sign-in's open connections within about 5 seconds.
- The server puts your login shell's `PATH` first, so with nvm, volta, asdf or mise it runs the same `node`, `claude` and `codex` as your terminal.
- Chats and scheduled tasks no longer fail when the local login is signed out: they start on your default account, or on a free account in auto-rotation. New Session shows the account and model the start will really use.
- Settings in the environment now use `DROPPEN_*` names (for example `DROPPEN_DATA_DIR`). The old `REMOTE_HAND_*` and `RH_*` names still work; the server notes once that they are deprecated.
- Security: a connection already open from a browser or phone is now also held to the seven-day limit on control. If none of your other devices has confirmed the device list for seven days, the computer closes it; before, only new connections were checked.

## v0.2.125 更新内容

### macOS 版 Droppen App

- **App 现在会自动更新。** 启动后以及之后每六小时检查一次新版本，在后台下载（按流量计费的网络下不下载），校验签名，在你下次退出时安装。菜单栏里的「重启以更新」，或窗口侧栏底部的提示，可以立即安装。0.2.124 及更早的版本不会自动更新：请手动下载一次这个 DMG，替换「应用程序」里的 Droppen；之后它就会自己保持最新。
- **首次使用引导。** 帮你准备好 Claude Code 和 Codex：安装或更新（官方安装脚本、Homebrew 或 npm，运行前先给你看完整命令）、使用这台 Mac 上已有的副本，并完成登录。然后问你在哪里用 Droppen：在手机和其它设备上也能用（在浏览器里把这台 Mac 登录到你的 Droppen 账户），或者只在这台 Mac 上用。最后在最近的项目文件夹或新建的练习文件夹里打开已填好的第一个会话，你按下发送之前什么都不会运行。可以「稍后再说」，之后在 设置 → 通用 → 设置引导 里重新打开。
- **在 App 里登录 Droppen**，在引导里或 设置 → Droppen 里都可以：会在浏览器中打开 droppen.app；在那里添加这台设备后，这台 Mac 就完成了登录，无需重启。
- **「登录时启动」改为 macOS 登录项：** 系统设置 → 登录项 中显示为 Droppen，App 移动或改名后依然有效。新安装默认开启；原有的登录时启动设置会自动迁移一次。
- **「在浏览器中打开」** 用一次性链接让浏览器直接登录。
- App 内置的服务启动时保持原来的端口，页面的登录状态和这台 Mac 的设备钥匙因此不会丢失；需要换端口时，新端口取自 18700–18759。
- 窗口里不再出现从命令行安装复制过来的两个空白屏幕，侧栏的名称提示也不再挡住窗口的关闭、最小化和缩放按钮。
- App 及其 DMG 附带 `LICENSE` 和 `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES`。
- App 现在需要 macOS 13 或更高版本。

### 网页（droppen.app 和设备上的本地页面）

- **iPhone 键盘：** 输入框不再被键盘挡住。页面不再自己测量键盘高度，改由 Safari 把输入框滚动到可见位置。
- **「我的设备」合成一页：** 顶部是用量（套餐、本期流量、云端存储），然后是这台设备的设置，下面是所有设备的统一列表，带「可运行 AI」「完整设备」标记，每台设备有自己的菜单。浏览器和手机以设备和浏览器命名，例如「iPhone · Safari」。「添加设备」会先问要添加哪一类。
- **「我的账户」** 是新页面（从侧栏和「我的设备」进入）：显示邮箱和登录方式。可以添加、重命名或移除通行密钥；设置、修改或移除密码；查看恢复词；退出登录。在一台设备上做的更改，会在你的其他设备上提示。用通行密钥登录的新手机或浏览器可以凭它加入，只要你的另一台设备通过常规配对批准。
- **添加的每个浏览器或手机都是完整设备：** 不再有「可管理账号」的选项。某台设备不接受这个浏览器时，页面会说明原因，而不是一直显示「连接中…」。
- **会话内容只追加。** Claude 思考时，状态栏在消息数旁边显示「思考中… ~50 tokens」，会话里不再出现一个高度不停跳动的临时框。
- **iPhone 和 Safari 上的设备钥匙：** 修复了手机配对后一刷新就连不上的问题，钥匙现在能保留下来。钥匙一时读不出来时，手机不再直接变成一台新设备：页面会重试；如果仍然打不开，会先征求你的同意，再把这个浏览器作为新设备使用。浏览器以新设备重新登录时，批准它的设备会提示顺手移除旧的那台。
- **中继：** 你每台可运行 AI 的设备现在即使还没有浏览器或手机用过中继，也会保持连着一个中继；所在中继下线时会立即换到另一个。「我的设备」新增账户设置「保留备用中继」，切换更快。
- 配对中止时会说明原因，并给出可行的下一步（例如登录超过 30 分钟后需要重新登录）。
- 电脑的「需要重新加入」警告会列出实际连接过它的设备，并且可以关闭。
- 新注册的账户直接使用当前的设备列表格式，不会再出现升级步骤。
- 各个下拉选择（模型、思考强度等）改用 App 自己的菜单，不再是系统菜单，并且紧贴着按钮弹出。
- 新建会话：问题、输入框和最近使用的文件夹排成居中的一栏；手机上输入框固定在底部，与会话页一致。
- 在 Droppen 还在选择账户时发送的消息，会按你按下时的内容原样发出；之后输入或添加的附件留在输入框里，切换 AI 工具会取消这次发送。
- 安全：停留在旧版设备列表上的标签页，不能再把其他标签页已验证的结果回滚；页面发现两份不同的设备列表时会停止。

### 服务端（`droppen`）

- **设备上的页面始终要求登录。** 没有用户账户的设备，不再对任何没有登录会话的人开放，即使来自 127.0.0.1 也一样。在设备上运行 `droppen open`（或用 App 的「在浏览器中打开」）；在其他地方，用一次性设置码（`droppen setup-code`）创建第一个账户，或在设备上运行 `droppen user add <name>`。用安装脚本安装的设备已经有账户，不受影响。
- 页面不能再被嵌入其他网站的框架中。这也意味着它无法在 VS Code 或 Cursor 的 Simple Browser 里打开，请使用真正的浏览器。
- 退出登录、撤销登录或删除账户后，该登录已打开的连接会在约 5 秒内关闭。
- 服务优先使用登录 shell 的 `PATH`，使用 nvm、volta、asdf 或 mise 时，运行的 `node`、`claude` 和 `codex` 与终端里一致。
- 本地登录已退出时，聊天和定时任务不再失败：会使用你的默认账号，或参与自动轮换的空闲账号启动。新建会话会显示实际将使用的账户和模型。
- 环境变量改用 `DROPPEN_*` 名称（例如 `DROPPEN_DATA_DIR`）。旧的 `REMOTE_HAND_*` 和 `RH_*` 名称仍然有效，服务会提示一次它们已弃用。
- 安全：已经打开的浏览器或手机连接，现在同样受七天控制期限约束。如果七天内没有你的其他设备确认过设备列表，电脑会关闭这条连接；以前只检查新建的连接。
